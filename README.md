# IPO Book Scanner

Research tool: place an Indian IPO subscription book (QIB / NII / retail / total multiples) against 1,400+ past
listings, see its framework bucket, how that bucket and its size band listed, and the 5 closest historical books.
The "How it works" tab explains the thinking and the backtest.

**For research purposes only. Not investment advice.**

Data refreshes on weekdays after bidding closes.
